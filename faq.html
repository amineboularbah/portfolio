What is Receipto?

Receipto is an iOS expense management application developed by Amine Boularbah. It uses optical character recognition (OCR) to automatically extract data from receipt photos and a Magic Email inbox feature that processes forwarded receipts automatically. The app supports 162+ currencies, exports to PDF, CSV, and Excel, and has over 400 App Store ratings.

What is Flura?

Flura is an iOS health tracking application developed by Amine Boularbah for people with chronic conditions including fibromyalgia, PCOS, endometriosis, long COVID, lupus, and ME/CFS. Users log daily symptoms in under 60 seconds. The app identifies patterns over time and generates a physician-formatted PDF report with a symptom timeline and appointment talking points. All data is encrypted and GDPR compliant.
